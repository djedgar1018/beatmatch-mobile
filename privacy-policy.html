Mix-Match Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Mix-Match ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by AIDE Holdings LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, and profile details when you create an account.

Profile Information: For DJs — stage name, bio, genres, hourly rate, location, portfolio media. For venues — business name, address, event types.

Booking Data: Event details, dates, payment information processed through Stripe.

Messages: Communications between DJs and venues through our platform.

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect DJs with venues for bookings
  • To process payments and manage contracts
  • To send booking notifications and updates
  • To improve our platform and user experience
  • To process subscription payments via Apple In-App Purchase

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. DJ profile information (name, bio, genres, location, rate) is visible to venue users to facilitate bookings. Venue contact information is shared with DJs for confirmed bookings.

Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Stripe and Apple (for subscriptions). We do not store credit card numbers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time through Settings → Delete Account, or by contacting us at support@mix-match.app.

Children's Privacy

Mix-Match is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
