MIX-MATCH — Book DJs. Get Booked. Get Paid.

Mix-Match is the DJ booking marketplace built for the culture. Whether you're a DJ looking for your next gig or a venue searching for the perfect sound — Mix-Match connects you directly. No middlemen. No redirects. No $10/month just to search.

── FOR DJs ──
• Get discovered by venues and event planners in your city
• Manage booking requests in one place — accept, decline, counter
• Auto-generated contracts protect you every time
• Get paid directly via Stripe — 80% goes to you
• Sell your mixes as downloadable tracks with the Mix Store
• Showcase your TikTok videos directly on your profile
• Three subscription tiers unlock featured placement and analytics

── FOR VENUES & EVENT PLANNERS ──
• Browse verified DJs by genre, location, rate, and availability
• Send booking requests in seconds — no account needed to browse
• Real-time messaging with DJs before you commit
• Digital contracts signed in-app — no paperwork
• Track all your bookings in one dashboard

── WHY MIX-MATCH ──
Other apps link you to Upwork or Fiverr and call it a day. Mix-Match owns the entire booking flow — from discovery to contract to payment — so you never leave the app wondering if your gig is confirmed.

• Direct booking — not an aggregator

DJs can upload and sell their recorded mixes directly to fans and venues. Set your price, choose your clip for preview, and earn passive income between gigs.
