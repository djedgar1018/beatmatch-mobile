Welcome to Beat Jump!

This is the first release of the Beat Jump DJ booking marketplace for iOS.

What's included in v1.0:
• Browse and discover DJs by genre and location
• Instant booking requests with real-time status updates
• In-app messaging between DJs and venues
• Mix Store — preview, purchase, and download DJ sets for offline playback
• DJ subscription plans (Starter, Pro, Elite) with Apple In-App Purchase
• Push notifications for booking requests, messages, and event reminders
• Beautiful dark-themed interface built for music professionals

We're just getting started — more features, more DJs, more music coming soon. Follow us and leave a review to help other users find Beat Jump.
