FEATURES TO TEST
----------------
1. DISCOVER (Home tab)
   - Browse the DJ listing — search by name or filter by genre
   - Tap a DJ card to open their full profile

2. DJ PROFILE
   - Scroll to see bio, genres, and stats
   - Tap "Mix Store" to see the mix purchase flow (demo mixes shown if no API data)
   - Tap "Book [DJ Name]" to submit a booking request

3. BOOKINGS (Bookings tab)
   - View pending requests and upcoming bookings
   - Tap a booking for full details

4. MESSAGES (Messages tab)
   - View conversation threads and send messages

5. SUBSCRIPTIONS (More tab)
   - Three subscription tiers: Starter ($29), Pro ($79), Elite ($149)
   - Plans use Apple In-App Purchase (auto-renewable subscriptions)
   - Tap "Restore Purchases" to restore prior subscriptions
   - Legal disclosure text is shown per App Store guidelines

6. MIX STORE (from DJ profile)
   - Preview 30-second clips (requires previewUrl on server)
   - "Buy & Download" opens external Stripe checkout (reader app exemption per App Store guidelines — users pay on the web, content available in app)
   - After purchase, users download the file to device for offline playback

7. LIBRARY (from Mix Store after download)
   - Offline audio playback with scrubber, skip controls

NOTES FOR REVIEWER
------------------
- In-app purchases require a Sandbox Apple ID for testing. The app correctly presents the StoreKit purchase sheet.
- The Mix Store purchase flow uses the reader app exemption (Apple Developer Program License Agreement section 3.3.3): users complete payment on the web, then download purchased content within the app.
- Push notifications require physical device; the demo account will receive test notifications upon booking events.
- The app requires internet connectivity for initial login and data fetching; downloaded mixes play offline.
